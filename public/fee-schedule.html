How to Read This Page

The table above groups the cost and limit categories that commonly apply. Your specific amounts depend on your plan documents and account type, and the figures that matter to you are shown in the portal at the moments they apply — before you confirm elections and on every statement afterward.

If a line item ever looks unfamiliar, member support can explain what it covers and why it applies to your account.

Nothing on this page changes the terms of your plan documents, and where a discrepancy exists the plan documents control. This summary exists to make the categories easier to understand at a glance before you dig into the formal language.

For answers to common questions about costs and contributions, see the FAQ page, or contact member support for detail specific to your account.

Related questions about costs and limits are covered in the FAQ and Resources pages as well.
