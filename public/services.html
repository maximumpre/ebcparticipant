Account Services

Everything you can do with your spending accounts in the portal.

Claims & Reimbursements

  • Submit claims: file reimbursement claims with itemized receipts and track them while they are reviewed.
  • Direct deposit: receive approved reimbursements straight to your bank account.
  • Receipt upload: photograph and upload documentation to substantiate card purchases.
  • Claim history: review past claims, decisions, and payment details in one record.

Cards & Payments

  • Benefits debit card: activate a new card and use it for eligible expenses wherever cards are accepted.
  • Card controls: review transactions and report a card that is lost or damaged.
  • Balances: see current balances for every account at a glance.

Account Management

Update contact and banking details, review eligible expense lists, and check the deadlines that apply to your plan — including year-end FSA submission windows and HSA contribution limits published each year.

Availability of specific features varies by account type and plan. Sign in for details that apply to you, or contact member support with questions.

Self-Service, Any Time

Every workflow in the portal is designed to be completed on your own schedule. Tasks that once required forms and phone calls now finish in minutes, with confirmation at each step and a record of what was submitted. When a request needs review, the portal shows the status so you are never left guessing where things stand.

Because these tools are available around the clock, you can handle time-sensitive matters without waiting for business hours. Guidance is built into each flow, and help is close by if a step is unclear.

The service catalog grows as needs evolve. Features are added based on the questions and requests members bring to support, so if a tool you expect is missing today it may well be on the way. Feedback through the Contact page directly shapes what gets built next.

Visit the Resources page for guides covering each workflow in depth, or head to the FAQ for quick answers to common questions about how these services work day to day.
