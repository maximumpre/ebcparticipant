Processing Timeframes

What to expect when you submit claims, card requests, and account changes.

Availability of specific features varies by account type and plan. Sign in for details that apply to you, or contact member support with questions.

What Affects Timing

Most delays come from incomplete submissions rather than queue length. Including everything a request asks for up front is the single best way to keep it moving, and the portal lists required items before you submit.

Timeframes above describe typical handling once a complete request is received. If a request is time-sensitive, note the deadline in your submission and support will prioritize accordingly.

External review steps, verification requirements, and delivery cycles outside the portal can add time beyond the windows above. Where that is the case, the status tracker names the party holding the request so you know exactly where things stand.

Common questions about request handling are answered on the FAQ page, and member support can clarify anything specific to your situation. When you plan around these windows, building in a small buffer for external steps keeps every deadline comfortable and stress free.

Planning around these windows is easiest when requests are submitted early in the week.
