How Requests Work

Requests submitted through the portal are recorded against your account with a timestamp and reference, so both you and support see the same record. Where a form requires documentation, the submission flow lists exactly what is needed before you finish, which prevents avoidable back-and-forth later.

After submission, status updates appear in the same place the request was made. If anything else is needed, a notice tells you what to add rather than leaving the request waiting silently.

Completed forms remain available in your account history after processing, so you always have a record of what was submitted and when. That history is also the fastest reference if the same request is needed again.

Questions about which form applies to your situation can be directed to member support through the Contact page before you submit anything.

Related guides are available on the Resources page for the requests members submit most often.
