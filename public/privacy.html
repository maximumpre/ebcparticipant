Our Privacy Statement

This Privacy Statement explains information Employee Benefits Corporation (“EBC”, “we,” “our,” “us”) collects and uses to deliver products and services. Those offerings include flexible spending accounts, health reimbursement arrangements, health savings accounts, lifestyle spending accounts, COBRA administration, and other employer-sponsored programs (each an “Account,” together the “Services”).

We may revise this statement at any time. Review it regularly. Using the Services, website, or mobile application means you accept this Privacy Statement. If you do not agree, do not use those channels. This statement, the website, and the mobile application are intended for people who live in the United States.

This statement does not cover third-party websites or apps that link to or from our sites, or sites that publish their own privacy notices. Our digital properties are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly solicit online data from, or market online to, children under 13.

Personal Information We Collect

To provide Services, we may collect:

  • Name, email, and physical address
  • Social Security Number (SSN) and date of birth
  • Phone number
  • Identifying information for dependents
  • Identifying information for people you authorize to use or view Account information
  • Account activity such as elections, balances, payment history, claims, reimbursements, distributions, and contributions
  • Banking and payment information
  • Employer-provided data needed for annual cafeteria plan nondiscrimination testing, including contribution amounts, compensation details, and ownership or officer status
  • Information described in the Cookies section below

How We Collect Your Personal Information

We collect personal information directly from you through the website, mobile application, or other forms, and indirectly from your employer, insurance company, or affiliates when needed to run the Services.

Cookies and Website Tracking

Cookies and analytics tools help us deliver services, answer inquiries, fix online issues, improve the site and experience, and measure quality programs. We may share this technical data with vendors that process it for those purposes. It typically does not reveal your name, contact details, or claim content, but may include IP address, browser and device traits, OS, language, referring URLs, device name, country, location, and how or when you use the site.

We do not sell this information for marketing. Security controls and vendor agreements help protect privacy. Related cookie choices are also described in theTerms of Use.

Log Files

IP addresses support website administration and are not linked to personally identifiable information in that use. Limited non-identifying device and OS data may support mobile application troubleshooting.

How We Use and Share Personal Information

We may use or share personal information to:

  • Authenticate identity and open, maintain, and administer Accounts
  • Process and pay benefit claims
  • Communicate about Services or Accounts
  • Make Pay Your Provider payments
  • Help prevent fraud, security incidents, and financial loss
  • Respond to information you request
  • Engage service providers that help deliver Services
  • Record transactions and communications
  • Meet legal, regulatory, administrative, or subpoena requirements
  • Follow your authorization or instructions
  • Otherwise lawfully operate the business, provide Services, or administer Accounts

We do not sell or rent personal information. We also do not “share” personal information as that term is used under California law for cross-context behavioral advertising. Sharing is limited to what this statement describes and what law permits.

Access is limited to employees, affiliates, and service providers who need the information to deliver Services, fight fraud or security incidents, or run the business lawfully. For employment-based Accounts, we may disclose Account details — such as type, balance, activity, and contributions — to a current or former employer. If you authorize family members or others, you are responsible for their Account actions; authorized users may see balances after standard authentication.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, Account type, and data category, you may have rights to know, access, update, or remove personal information. Deletion requests often require coordination with a current or former employer when data relates to an employer benefit plan. We typically cannot delete that information unless the employer authorizes it. You may also update some details in your online account or by emailingparticipantservices@ebcflex.com.

Rights of California Residents

Employee Benefits Corporation acts as a service provider under California privacy laws when it collects personal information for employers that administer employee benefit plans. Deletion generally requires an employer or former employer instruction. Personal information is not disclosed to third parties for their direct marketing.

Information Security

Physical, administrative, and technical safeguards protect personal information against unauthorized use or disclosure. Electronic channels cannot be guaranteed completely secure — for example, transmissions over the internet may be intercepted. Protect your Account with a strong, unique password and keep that password confidential.

Email Communications

Email is used for Account transactions, service updates, education, and related notices. Service emails typically cannot be opted out of. Marketing emails may include unsubscribe links.

Push Notifications

The mobile application may send push notifications about activity such as transaction confirmations, claim updates, or alerts. Sensitive Account details are not placed in notification content. Manage or disable notifications in device settings. Secure vendors may process limited device identifiers solely to deliver notifications. Delivery and response metrics may be used to improve services and are not used for unrelated purposes.

Applications for Employment with Employee Benefits Corporation

Candidate information — such as resumes, work history, and references — may be collected for open roles and used to match skills, experience, and education to positions.

Gramm-Leach-Bliley Notice

A separate Gramm-Leach-Bliley privacy notice describes how nonpublic personal financial information is collected, shared, and protected. Review that notice relating to personal financial information for additional detail.
