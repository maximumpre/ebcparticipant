Frequently Asked Questions

8 answers
How do I sign in to EBC Flex?

Open the portal sign-in page and enter the credentials provided by your employer or plan administrator. Multi-factor verification may be required to protect your health and account information.

How do I submit a reimbursement claim?

Sign in, open the claims section, and follow the claim submission flow. Attach an itemized receipt or explanation of benefits; complete documentation is the fastest route to a decision.

Where can I check my account balances?

Current balances for every account appear on your dashboard after sign in, along with recent card transactions and pending claims.

How do I activate my benefits debit card?

Card activation is available in the card section after sign in. Once activated, the card can be used for eligible expenses wherever cards are accepted.

What happens if a card purchase needs a receipt?

Some transactions require substantiation. A notice appears in your account when documentation is needed — upload the receipt there and the transaction stays in good standing.

Can I get reimbursements by direct deposit?

Yes. Set up direct deposit in the banking section and approved reimbursements are sent straight to your account.

What is the deadline for FSA claims?

Deadlines depend on your specific plan. Your plan materials define the submission window and any grace period or rollover rules that apply.

How do I contact member support?

Support options are on the Contact page, including a secure email channel. Signed-in requests carry full account context for faster help.
