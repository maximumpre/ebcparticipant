Frequently Asked Questions

8 answers
How do I sign in to my EBC Flex account?

Open the participant sign-in page and enter the username and password provided by your employer or plan administrator. Multi-factor verification may be required to protect your health and account information.

What should I do if I cannot access my account?

Use Forgot Username or Reset Password on the sign-in page to verify your identity and restore access. If recovery is not possible with your registered contact details, contact member support from the Contact page.

How do I submit a reimbursement claim?

After sign in, open Claims and follow the submission flow. Attach an itemized receipt or explanation of benefits; complete documentation is the fastest route to a decision.

Where can I check my account balances?

Current balances for every account appear on your dashboard after sign in, along with recent card transactions and pending claims.

How do I activate my benefits debit card?

Card activation is available in the card section after sign in. Once activated, the card can be used for eligible expenses wherever cards are accepted.

What happens if a card purchase needs a receipt?

Some transactions require substantiation. A notice appears in your account when documentation is needed — upload the receipt there and the transaction stays in good standing.

Can I get reimbursements by direct deposit?

Yes. Set up direct deposit in the banking section after sign in and approved reimbursements are sent straight to your account.

How is my account information protected?

Sessions use TLS encryption, multi-layered validation, and custodial security controls so health and account data stay confidential. Review the Security page for member best practices.
